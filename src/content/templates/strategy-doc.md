---
## When to use
At the start of a quarter or a new product area, or as the P1 Week 3 deliverable. Aim for 2 pages.

## How to fill it
1. Start with the **diagnosis**: the core challenge in the market or for users. A strategy without a diagnosis is a wish list.
2. Make **choices** explicit, including what you are saying no to.
3. Link each bet to a metric.
4. Keep the roadmap as Now / Next / Later themes, not dates.

# Strategy: FlatSplit (first year)

## 1. Vision
Shared living in India where money never strains a friendship.

## 2. Diagnosis
- **Users:** Flatmates settle late because asking is awkward; tracking tools are dropped within 2 months (discovery, n=12 + 64 survey).
- **Market:** ~1.5–2 crore young professionals in shared housing in metros (estimate). Splitwise is known but limits free use; most people fall back to WhatsApp.
- **Our position:** UPI-native from day one, focused only on flats, not trips.
- **Core challenge:** making settlement happen without anyone acting as the “collector”.

## 3. Target segment
Working professionals 22–30 in 3–4 person flats in Bengaluru. They have recurring shared costs (rent, cook, Wi-Fi), high UPI usage and strong word of mouth in office groups.

## 4. Strategic choices
| We will | We will not | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Focus on recurring flat expenses | Build trip splitting | Recurring costs create monthly habit |
| Use UPI deep links | Hold or move money ourselves | Avoids regulatory and trust burden |
| Grow through flat invites | Spend on paid ads in year one | Each flat brings 2–3 users for free |

## 5. Bets
| Bet | Hypothesis | Metric | Signal by |
|---|---|---|---|
| Monthly settle-up summary | Neutral app reminder raises settling | % groups fully settled (31% → 45%) | 8 weeks |
| Recurring expenses (rent, cook) | Auto-added costs keep groups active | Month-2 group retention (40% → 55%) | 10 weeks |
| AI expense entry (“paid 1200 for groceries”) | Lower logging effort | Expenses logged per group per week | 12 weeks |

## 6. Roadmap themes
- **Now:** settle faster (summary, one-tap UPI).
- **Next:** effortless logging (recurring costs, AI entry).
- **Later:** move-in / move-out (deposits, handovers).

## 7. Risks and what would change our mind
- Reminders feel naggy → opt-out rate above 3%.
- Users only want trip splitting → more than 30% of new groups named “trip”.
