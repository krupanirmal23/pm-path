## When to use
Before you start outreach (Week 12). Recruiters search by keywords in the headline and read the first 3 lines of About.

## How to fill it
- **Headline (≤ 220 characters):** target role | proof | edge. Avoid “Aspiring” on its own; show what you have done.
- **About:** hook → background → proof (projects with numbers) → what you want → how to reach you.
- Write in first person, short paragraphs, no buzzwords without evidence.
- Add portfolio links to the Featured section.
