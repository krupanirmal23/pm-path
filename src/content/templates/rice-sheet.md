## When to use
When you have more ideas than capacity and need a transparent way to rank them. Use the **RICE Calculator** in the Toolkit for automatic scoring and CSV export.

## How to fill it
- **Reach:** users affected per time period (e.g. per quarter). Use real numbers.
- **Impact:** 3 = massive, 2 = high, 1 = medium, 0.5 = low, 0.25 = minimal.
- **Confidence:** 100% = data, 80% = some evidence, 50% = gut feel.
- **Effort:** person-months (or person-weeks; be consistent).
- **Score** = Reach × Impact × Confidence ÷ Effort.
Treat the score as a conversation starter. Write down why you overrode it, if you do.

# RICE: {area}, {quarter}

| Idea | Reach (/qtr) | Impact | Confidence | Effort (person-months) | Score | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| | | | | | | |

**Assumptions behind reach numbers:** {source}
**Decision:** {what we will do first and why; any overrides}

<!-- example -->
# RICE: FlatSplit, Q4

| Idea | Reach (/qtr) | Impact | Confidence | Effort (person-months) | Score | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Monthly settle-up summary | 6,000 | 2 | 80% | 1.5 | 6,400 | Strong interview evidence |
| Recurring expenses | 4,500 | 1 | 80% | 1 | 3,600 | Requested by 5 of 12 |
| AI expense entry | 6,000 | 1 | 50% | 3 | 1,000 | Unproven accuracy |
| Dark mode | 6,000 | 0.25 | 80% | 0.5 | 2,400 | Low impact, cheap |
| Trip splitting | 1,500 | 1 | 50% | 2 | 375 | Off-strategy |

**Assumptions behind reach numbers:** 6,000 monthly active users; 75% have recurring costs; 25% take trips per quarter.
**Decision:** Build the settle-up summary first. Dark mode scores above recurring expenses but we will do recurring expenses next because it supports the retention bet in our strategy; dark mode goes into a polish sprint.
