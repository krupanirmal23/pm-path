## When to use
For APM / PM applications. Keep one master version and tailor the summary and top bullets for each role.

## How to fill it
1. **One page**, simple layout, no columns or tables (ATS systems mis-read them).
2. Bullet formula: **action verb + what you did + measurable result**.
3. Reframe past work in product language: users, problems, decisions, outcomes.
4. Put **Projects** above Education if they are your strongest proof.
5. Match keywords from the job description truthfully.
