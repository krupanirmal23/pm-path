## When to use
- **No reply:** follow up once after 4–5 working days. Stop after the second follow-up.
- **After an interview:** send a thank-you within 24 hours.
- **After a referral:** thank them and keep them updated.

## How to fill it
Add something new each time (a link, an update), never just “bumping this”. Keep it to 3–4 lines.

<!-- template -->
## Follow-up (no reply)
Hi {name}, following up on my note about the {role} role. Since then I {new thing: published a case study / shipped an update}: {link}. Happy to share more if helpful. Thanks!

## Thank-you after interview
Hi {name}, thank you for the conversation today about {specific topic}. I especially enjoyed {detail}. It made me more excited about {team goal}. {Optional: one extra thought on a question you discussed.} Looking forward to next steps.

## Thank-you after referral
Hi {name}, thank you so much for the referral. I’ll keep you posted on how it goes. If I can ever help with {something relevant}, just let me know.
