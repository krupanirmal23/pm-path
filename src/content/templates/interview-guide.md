## When to use
Before every round of discovery interviews. Reuse the same core questions across 5–8 interviews so you can compare answers.

## How to fill it
1. Write one **learning goal** and 2–3 assumptions you want to test.
2. Ask about **the last time** something happened, never “would you…”.
3. Avoid leading words (“frustrating”, “easy”). Let them choose the adjectives.
4. Leave 40% of time for follow-ups: “Tell me more”, “What happened next?”, “Why was that?”.

**Who:** {screener criteria} · **Length:** 30 min · **Recording consent:** yes/no

## Intro (2 min)
“Thanks for your time. I’m learning how people {topic}. There are no right answers, and I’m not selling anything. Can I record this for my notes only?”

## Warm-up (3 min)
- Tell me a bit about {context}.

## The last time (15 min)
- Tell me about the last time you {behaviour}.
- What happened just before that?
- How did you {do it}? Walk me through it.
- What was the hardest part?
- What did you try to fix it? What did that cost you (time, money)?

## Current tools (5 min)
- What do you use today for {job}? What do you like and dislike?

## Wrap-up (5 min)
- If you could wave a magic wand, what would change? (note: treat as a hint, not a spec)
- Who else should I talk to?

# Interview guide: how flatmates share expenses

**Learning goal:** Decide whether month-end settling is painful enough to build for.
**Assumptions to test:**
1. Flatmates settle late because they don’t know exact amounts.
2. Asking for money feels awkward.
3. People already use spreadsheets or apps and drop them.

**Who:** 22–30, sharing a flat in Bengaluru with 2+ people for 3+ months · **Length:** 30 min · **Recording consent:** yes

## Intro (2 min)
“Thanks for your time. I’m learning how flatmates manage shared costs. There are no right answers and I’m not selling anything. Can I record this for my notes only?”

## Warm-up (3 min)
- Tell me about your flat. Who do you live with and how long?

## The last time (15 min)
- Tell me about the last time you and your flatmates settled money.
- What expenses were involved?
- How did you work out who owed what? Walk me through it.
- How long did it take from the first expense to everyone being paid?
- Was there any moment that felt uncomfortable? What happened?
- Have you ever just not asked for money you were owed? Tell me about it.

## Current tools (5 min)
- What do you use today: WhatsApp, Splitwise, a sheet, memory? What made you start or stop?

## Wrap-up (5 min)
- If you could change one thing about how your flat handles money, what would it be?
- Do you know someone in a bigger flat (4+) I could speak to?
