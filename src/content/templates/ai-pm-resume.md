## When to use
For roles that mention LLMs, GenAI, ML or “AI product”. Start from your PM resume and change the summary, project order and skills.

## How to fill it
1. Lead the summary with **AI shipping evidence** (users, eval scores).
2. Move your AI project **to the top** and give it 3 bullets.
3. Show the AI PM vocabulary through real work: evals, failure analysis, cost per user, guardrails, human-in-the-loop.
4. Don’t claim ML engineering skills you don’t have. Show judgment instead.
