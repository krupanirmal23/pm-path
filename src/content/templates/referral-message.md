## When to use
For outreach to employees at target companies (aim for 5 a day). Referrals get far higher callback rates than cold applications.

## How to fill it
The 5-part structure:
1. **Personal hook:** why them (shared college, their post, their team).
2. **Who you are** in one line.
3. **Proof** relevant to their company.
4. **Specific ask** with the job link or ID. Make it easy to say yes.
5. **Easy out** and thanks.
Keep it under 100 words. LinkedIn connection notes are limited to about 300 characters, so use the short version there.

<!-- template -->
## Full message (InMail / after connecting)
Hi {name},

{hook: why you’re writing to them specifically}

I’m {one line about you}. {proof relevant to their company}.

I’m applying for {role} ({job link/ID}). Would you be open to referring me? I’ve attached my resume and a 1-page case study so it takes you two minutes.

Totally understand if it’s not possible. Thanks either way!
{your name}

## Short connection note (≤ 300 characters)
Hi {name}, {hook}. I’m {who you are} applying for {role} at {company}. Would love to connect and ask a quick question about the team.

<!-- example -->
## Full message (InMail / after connecting)
Hi Aditi,

I saw your post on how Razorpay’s payments team runs weekly customer calls. It’s the habit I try to copy in my own projects.

I’m a former Technical Marketing Engineer at Palo Alto Networks moving into product. I recently built FlatSplit, a UPI-based settle-up flow for flatmates, from 12 user interviews to a tested prototype.

I’m applying for the APM role on the Payments team (Job ID 48213). Would you be open to referring me? My resume and a 1-page case study are attached so it takes you two minutes.

Totally understand if it’s not possible. Thanks either way!
Krupa

## Short connection note (≤ 300 characters)
Hi Aditi, loved your post on weekly customer calls at Razorpay. I’m an ex-Palo Alto Networks TME moving into product and applying for the Payments APM role. Would love to connect and ask a quick question about the team.
