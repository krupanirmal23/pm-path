## What it is

**Loom** is a screen and camera recording tool. You record yourself (camera, screen or both), and Loom gives you a shareable link instantly. The free plan has limits on video length and the number of videos (check current limits); when you hit the limit, delete old practice videos.

## Why PMs use it

Recording practice answers is the fastest way to improve interview delivery. You’ll hear rambling, filler words and missing structure that you can’t notice while speaking. You can also share recordings with a mentor or peer for async feedback. Many PMs also use Loom at work for quick product walkthroughs.

## Account setup (free plan)

1. Go to loom.com → **Get Loom for free**; sign up with Google.
2. Install the **Chrome extension** or desktop app (or use the web recorder).
3. Allow camera and microphone permissions.
(Alternative: your phone’s camera app or Google Meet recording if available works too.)

## Walkthrough: the practice loop

### 1. Set up
1. Open PM Path **Interview Prep → Mock interview** in one tab; draw a random question in your target category.
2. Open a blank doc or a whiteboard (Excalidraw) to write your structure, just as you would in a real interview.
3. Start Loom: choose **Screen + Camera**, select the tab or window with your notes.

### 2. Record a full answer
1. Read the question aloud.
2. Answer as if in a real interview: signpost, clarify (state assumptions), structure, check-in phrases (“does this segment make sense?”), metrics, summary.
3. Keep to the time limit shown by the Mock Interview timer.
4. Stop recording.

### 3. Review (the important part)
1. Watch at **1.5x speed**.
2. Score yourself with the Mock Interview rubric (structure, user focus, creativity, prioritisation, metrics, communication; 1–4 each).
3. Note: filler words, unexplained silences, places you jumped ahead, whether you reached metrics and summary.
4. Write one specific habit to fix next time.
5. Log the mock in PM Path (question, category, scores, verdict, notes).

### 4. Redo the weakest part
1. Re-record only the weakest section (e.g., solutions brainstorm) for 3–5 minutes.
2. Compare. Improvement on one section at a time compounds quickly.

### 5. Get feedback (optional)
1. Click **Share → Copy link**; set it so only people with the link can view.
2. Send to a PM mentor or peer with a specific request: “Could you rate my structure and tell me where you lost track?”
3. Use Loom’s timestamped comments to see exactly where feedback applies.

## Tips

- **Camera on** for at least some practice: you’ll notice body language and eye contact.
- **Record your 90-second “Tell me about yourself”** weekly until it’s natural.
- **Delete old videos** to stay within free limits; keep one “before” and one “after” for motivation.
- **Don’t over-edit**; the goal is realistic practice, not polished videos.
- **Privacy**: don’t record confidential employer information.
