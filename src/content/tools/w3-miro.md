## What it is

**Miro** is your online whiteboard from Weeks 1–2. This week you’ll draw two strategy visuals: a **competitor positioning map** and an **impact vs. effort 2x2**.

## Why PMs use it

Strategy discussions go faster with a picture. A positioning map shows at a glance where competitors sit and where a gap might be. A 2x2 lets a team sort many ideas in minutes. Both make great images for your strategy doc and portfolio.

## Account setup (free plan)

Use your existing account. If you’ve used all 3 free boards, export an old board as an image (⋯ → Export → Image) and delete or reuse it.

## Walkthrough part 1: positioning map

### 1. Draw the axes
1. Create a frame (press **F**) named “P1 Positioning map”.
2. Press **L** (line) and draw a horizontal line across the middle, then a vertical line. Hold **Shift** for straight lines.
3. Add arrowheads: select a line → in the toolbar, choose an arrow end style.
4. Label the axes with text boxes (**T**). Pick two dimensions your research shows users care about, e.g., “Effort to log expenses (high → low)” and “Settles real money (no → yes)”.

### 2. Plot competitors
1. Press **O** for a circle shape. Add one circle per alternative (Splitwise, Sheets, WhatsApp + UPI, UPI split request, “absorb it”).
2. Type the name inside each circle. Use a different pastel fill for each.
3. Place each based on your evidence (reviews and your own use). Add a small sticky note next to each with the evidence: “14 of 50 reviews: ‘too much manual entry’”.

### 3. Mark the gap
1. Draw a dashed rectangle (select a rectangle → border style **dashed**) around the empty area that matches your positioning.
2. Label it with your product name and a short positioning line.

## Walkthrough part 2: impact vs. effort 2x2

### 1. Make the grid
1. Create a new frame “P1 Impact vs Effort”.
2. Draw a large square, then a horizontal and vertical line through the middle to make four quadrants.
3. Label: top-left **Quick wins**, top-right **Big bets**, bottom-left **Fill-ins**, bottom-right **Money pits**. Axis labels: “Effort →” at the bottom, “Impact →” on the left.
4. Optional: fill quadrants with light colours (mint, sky, butter, pink) and send them to the back (right-click → **Send to back**), then lock them (right-click → **Lock**).

### 2. Add ideas
1. Paste your RICE idea list into sticky notes (one per idea).
2. **Place effort first**: ask your technical friend to drag each note left/right.
3. **Then impact**: you move notes up/down based on research.
4. Push anything in the middle to one side.

### 3. Capture decisions
1. Circle quick wins with a pen (**P**).
2. Add a sticky with next steps: “Quick wins → Now; Big bets → de-risking tests; Money pits → Won’t”.
3. Compare with your RICE ranking. Where do they disagree? Note why.

### 4. Export
1. Select each frame → **⋯** → **Export** → **Image** (or PDF).
2. Add both images to your Notion strategy doc.

## Tips

- **Same colour = same competitor** across all your boards.
- **Use relative placement**: ask “is A more effort than B?” rather than guessing absolute values.
- **Evidence stickies** next to plotted items make the map credible.
- **Time-box** the 2x2 to 15–20 minutes; it’s a fast sorting tool.
- **Duplicate frames** (Ctrl/Cmd + D) to keep a “before discussion” and “after discussion” version.
