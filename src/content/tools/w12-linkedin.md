## What it is

**LinkedIn** is the professional network recruiters and hiring managers use most in India. The free account is enough for profile building, job alerts, posting and messaging connections (InMail to non-connections is a paid feature; connection requests with notes are free, with limits on note length and weekly invitations).

## Why PMs use it

This week you’ll reposition your profile for product roles, find referrers at 25 target companies, and send personalised connection requests and messages. It’s also where your build-in-public posts live.

## Account setup (free plan)

You likely have an account. Make sure your email is verified and your profile is set to public visibility (**Settings → Visibility → Edit your public profile**).

## Walkthrough

### 1. Update the profile (follow the LinkedIn positioning lesson)
1. **Photo and banner:** click the camera icons on your profile.
2. **Headline and current position:** click the pencil next to your name. Write your new headline.
3. **Custom URL:** on your profile, **Edit public profile & URL** (right side) → edit to `linkedin.com/in/krupa-yoursurname`.
4. **About:** Add profile section → About. Paste your About text.
5. **Featured:** Add profile section → Recommended → Add featured → add links (portfolio, P4 app) and posts.
6. **Projects:** Add profile section → Recommended/Additional → Projects. Add P1–P4 with one-line results and links. Associate them with an honest context (e.g., “Independent product projects”).
7. **Skills:** add up to 50; reorder so your top 3 relevant skills show first.
8. **Open to work:** Open to → Finding a new job → titles (Associate Product Manager, Product Manager, AI Product Manager, Product Analyst), location Bengaluru, visibility **Recruiters only** if you prefer discretion.

### 2. Set job alerts
1. **Jobs** → search “Associate Product Manager”, location Bengaluru → **Set alert** (daily).
2. Repeat for “Product Manager AI”, “Product Analyst”, “Technical Product Manager”.

### 3. Find referrers
1. Search: `"product manager" [Company]` → filter **People** → **Current company**.
2. Add filters: **School** (your college) for alumni; **Past company** (Palo Alto Networks) for ex-colleagues.
3. For each of your 25 companies, save 2 names in the Job Tracker’s referral contact field.

### 4. Send connection requests with notes
1. Click **Connect** → **Add a note** (keep within the note length limit; usually short).
2. Example: “Hi Sneha, fellow [college] alum and TME moving into product. Enjoyed your post on pricing experiments. Would love to connect.”
3. After they accept, send the referral message (Referral outreach lesson) as a regular message.
4. Pace yourself: about 5–10 personalised requests a day; LinkedIn limits weekly invitations.

### 5. Post and engage
1. Click **Start a post**. Use the draft from your LinkedIn lesson exercise. Add one image (eval chart, diagram).
2. Reply to every comment in the first few hours.
3. Spend 10 minutes a day commenting thoughtfully on 2–3 posts by PMs at target companies.

### 6. Track
1. Log outreach in PM Path’s outreach counter daily (target 5).
2. Move companies across the Job Tracker board as you apply and get referrals.

## Tips

- **Personalise every note**; generic requests are often ignored.
- **Don’t paste your resume** into messages to strangers; offer a link once there’s a conversation.
- **Check Settings → Data privacy** if you want your profile changes not broadcast to your network (“Share profile updates”).
- **Avoid automation tools** that mass-message; they risk account restrictions.
- **Keep consistency** with your resume (titles, dates, numbers).
