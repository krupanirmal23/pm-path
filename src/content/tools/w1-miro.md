## What it is

**Miro** is an online whiteboard: an infinite canvas where you can add sticky notes, shapes, arrows, text and images, and collaborate in real time.

## Why PMs use it

PMs use whiteboards for anything visual and messy: user journeys, affinity maps (Week 2), opportunity trees, competitor maps (Week 3), workshop brainstorming and retrospectives. Teams across Bengaluru use Miro, FigJam or similar tools; the skills transfer directly.

## Account setup (free plan)

1. Go to miro.com and click **Sign up free**. Use your Google account.
2. When asked about your team, choose **Just me** or skip.
3. The free plan gives you **3 editable boards**. That’s enough if you reuse boards: one for Week 1 journeys, one for Week 2 research, one for Week 3 strategy. You can export old boards as images and clear them if you need space.

## A quick primer: what is a user journey map?

A **user journey map** shows the steps a user takes to complete a job, and how they feel at each step. It has four rows:

1. **Stages:** the big phases (e.g., Decide → Order → Wait → Receive).
2. **Actions:** what the user does at each stage.
3. **Thoughts and feelings:** what they’re thinking, with a simple mood line (happy, neutral, frustrated).
4. **Pain points and opportunities:** where things go wrong, and ideas to fix them.

## Walkthrough: map the Swiggy dinner journey

### 1. Create the board
1. Click **+ New board** → **Blank board**. Name it **Week 1 – Journeys**.
2. Press **T** to add a text box. Type the title: “Swiggy: ordering dinner after work”.

### 2. Draw the stage columns
1. Press **S** for shapes, pick a rectangle, and draw one wide header box. Type **Decide**.
2. Select it, press **Ctrl/Cmd + D** to duplicate, and move it right. Repeat for **Order**, **Pay**, **Wait**, **Receive and eat**.
3. Down the left side, add row labels with text boxes: **Actions**, **Thoughts**, **Feeling**, **Pain points**, **Opportunities**.

### 3. Fill in with sticky notes
1. Press **N** for a sticky note. Use **yellow** for actions, **blue** for thoughts, **pink** for pain points and **green** for opportunities (change colour in the note’s toolbar).
2. Fill each column from your own experience and the reviews you mined. Examples:
   - Decide → Action: “Scroll home feed for 5 minutes.” Thought: “Nothing new looks good.” Pain: “Too many choices, no memory of what I liked.” Opportunity: “Show dishes I rated highly.”
   - Pay → Pain: “Surprise fees at checkout.”
   - Wait → Pain: “ETA changes from 25 to 45 minutes without explanation.”
3. Keep notes short: one idea per sticky, under ten words.

### 4. Add the emotion line
1. Press **L** for a line (or use the pen tool, **P**).
2. Draw a line across the Feeling row that rises at good moments and dips at bad ones.
3. Circle the two lowest points. These are your best candidates for the teardown’s “three problems”.

### 5. Export and use it
1. Click the **Share/Export** icon → **Export this board** → **Save as image**.
2. Paste the image into your Swiggy teardown in Notion under **Key flow**.
3. Duplicate the frame and repeat for CRED or Zerodha Kite if you have time.

## Tips

- **Use frames** (press **F**) around each journey so you can present them one at a time.
- **Zoom with Ctrl/Cmd + scroll**; press **Shift + 1** to fit everything on screen.
- **Lock** background shapes (right-click → Lock) so you don’t drag them by accident.
- **Colour has meaning.** Keep the same colour code on every board.
- **Real data beats imagination.** Put a real review quote on at least one pain-point sticky per stage.
