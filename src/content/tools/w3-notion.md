## What it is

**Notion**, your learning hub from Week 1. This week you’ll use it to write a **2-page strategy document** for P1 that pulls together your research, competitive analysis, priorities and roadmap.

## Why PMs use it

Strategy docs are how PMs align leadership and teams. In many companies, important decisions start with a written doc that people read before a meeting. Writing clearly and concisely is one of the most valued PM skills, and your strategy doc is proof you have it.

## Account setup (free plan)

Use your existing workspace. Under **Projects** in your PM Path Hub, create a page called **P1 – [your topic]** if you haven’t already. Your discovery report from Week 2 should live there too.

## Walkthrough: the strategy doc

### 1. Create the page and structure
1. Inside **P1**, create a page: “P1 Strategy – v1”.
2. Add a **callout** at the top (`/callout`) with a one-sentence summary: “We’ll win [user] by [how], starting with [first bet], measured by [goal].”
3. Add these headings (`/h2`):
   1. **Vision** (1–2 sentences)
   2. **Diagnosis** (what’s going on, from research and competitors)
   3. **Where we’ll play** (target user and job)
   4. **How we’ll win** (positioning statement + why we can win)
   5. **Big bets** (2–3 coherent actions)
   6. **What we won’t do** (3+ explicit no’s with reasons)
   7. **Goals / OKRs** (1–2)
   8. **Roadmap** (Now / Next / Later)
   9. **Risks and open questions**
   10. **Appendix** (links: RICE sheet, Miro boards, discovery report)

### 2. Keep it to 2 pages
1. Write the first draft freely, then cut. Aim for ~800–1,000 words in the main body.
2. Move supporting detail into **toggles** (`/toggle`) or the Appendix.
3. Use bullet points and short sentences. Bold the one key phrase in each section.

### 3. Embed your work
1. **Positioning map and 2x2:** drag the exported Miro images in, or type `/embed` and paste the Miro board link (the board must be shared with view access).
2. **RICE sheet:** type `/embed`, paste the Google Sheets link. Or paste a small table of the top 5 ideas with `/table`.
3. **Roadmap:** create a simple table (`/table`) with columns **Goal | Now | Next | Later**.

### 4. Add a decision log (optional but impressive)
1. At the bottom, add a `/table` named **Decision log** with columns: **Date | Decision | Options considered | Why | Owner**.
2. Log at least the “why #6 waits” decision.

### 5. Review and publish
1. Read it aloud. Anything you stumble on, rewrite.
2. Ask one person to read only the callout and headings. Can they explain your strategy back to you? If not, sharpen.
3. **Share → Publish**. Put the link in the Week 3 deliverable field.

## Tips

- **Lead with the answer** (the callout), then support it.
- **Every claim needs a source**: a quote count, a review count, a RICE score.
- **Write “won’t do” in full sentences** with reasons; it’s the most revealing section.
- **Use the Strategy doc template** in PM Path’s Templates page as a starting point.
- **Version it**: duplicate the page as “v2” after feedback rather than overwriting.
