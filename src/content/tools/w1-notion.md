## What it is

**Notion** is an all-in-one workspace for notes, documents and simple databases. Pages can contain text, images, tables, checklists and embedded files, and any page can be published as a public web page with one click.

## Why PMs use it

Many startups use Notion as their company wiki: PRDs, meeting notes, roadmaps and team docs all live there. Being fluent in Notion makes you useful from day one. For you, it’s also a free way to publish teardowns and, later, your whole portfolio.

## Account setup (free plan)

1. Go to notion.so and click **Get Notion free**.
2. Sign up with your Google account. Choose **For myself** when asked how you’ll use it.
3. Skip the templates offered during onboarding. You’ll build your own structure.
4. The free plan is enough for everything in this roadmap: unlimited pages and blocks for one person, and public page sharing.
5. Optional: install the desktop app and the mobile app so you can capture notes anywhere.

## Walkthrough: this week’s setup

You’ll create a learning hub with four sections and a teardown template.

### 1. Create the hub page
1. In the left sidebar, click **+ New page**. Title it **PM Path Hub**.
2. Pick an icon (click **Add icon**) so it’s easy to spot.
3. Type `/heading 2` and press Enter to add a heading. Add four headings: **Weekly log**, **Teardowns**, **Projects**, **Interview prep**.

### 2. Create a Weekly log database
1. Under **Weekly log**, type `/table` and choose **Table – inline**.
2. Rename the columns: **Week** (title), **Dates** (type: Date), **Hours** (type: Number), **Shipped** (type: Text), **Hard** (type: Text), **Next** (type: Text).
3. Add a row for Week 1. Fill it in on Saturday. This mirrors the Weekly Review page in PM Path and gives you something to show in interviews.

### 3. Build a teardown template
1. Under **Teardowns**, type `/page` to create a sub-page called **Teardown template**.
2. Add these headings: **Who it’s for**, **Core job**, **Key flow**, **Three problems**, **One fix**, **Metric and guardrail**, **Business model**, **Evidence (reviews and screenshots)**.
3. Under **Key flow**, type `/numbered list` and add placeholder steps.
4. Under **Evidence**, type `/toggle` to create collapsible sections for screenshots, so the page stays readable.
5. Duplicate the template three times (hover over the page in the sidebar → **⋯** → **Duplicate**). Rename them **Swiggy**, **CRED** and **Zerodha Kite**.

### 4. Add screenshots
1. Take screenshots on your phone of each step in the key flow.
2. Send them to your laptop (Google Photos, WhatsApp Web or email to yourself).
3. Drag the images into the **Key flow** section. Click an image and choose **Caption** to label each step.
4. Tip: type `/columns` and choose 3 columns to place screenshots side by side.

### 5. Publish a teardown
1. Open a finished teardown page. Click **Share** (top right) → **Publish** tab → **Publish**.
2. Turn **off** “Allow editing” and “Allow comments”, and turn **off** “Search engine indexing” until you’re happy with it.
3. Copy the public link. Paste it into the deliverable link field on the Week 1 page in PM Path.

## Tips

- **Use `/` for everything.** Type `/` anywhere to see every block type.
- **Keep one idea per block.** It makes moving things around with drag handles easy.
- **Use toggles for long evidence** so readers see your argument first and details on demand.
- **Use callouts** (`/callout`) for your one-line key insight at the top of each teardown.
- **Name pages consistently**: “Teardown – Swiggy – Nov 2026”. Future-you will thank you.
- **Don’t over-design.** Recruiters skim. Clear headings and screenshots beat colour and emoji.
