## What it is

**Claude** (claude.ai) and **ChatGPT** (chatgpt.com) are AI assistants built on large language models. You type a message and they reply in natural language. Both have free plans.

## Why PMs use it

PMs use AI assistants to draft documents, summarise interviews, brainstorm options, write SQL, and pressure-test their thinking. This week you’ll use them as a **study partner**: a patient examiner who asks you questions and critiques your answers. Retrieval practice (answering questions from memory) is one of the most effective ways to learn.

> [!WARNING]
> AI assistants can be confidently wrong (you’ll learn why in Week 9). Use them to *practise*, and check facts against your lessons in PM Path. Never paste anything private or confidential into them.

## Account setup (free plan)

1. Go to claude.ai or chatgpt.com and sign up with Google.
2. Verify your phone number if asked.
3. The free tiers have daily message limits. That’s plenty for 20–30 minutes of quizzing a day. If you hit a limit on one, switch to the other.

## Walkthrough: daily 20-minute self-quiz

### 1. The examiner prompt
Copy this prompt and fill in the topic:

```
You are a strict but kind product management interviewer.
Today's topic: [the four product risks].

Rules:
1. Ask me ONE question at a time. Wait for my answer.
2. Start easy, then get harder. Mix definitions, examples and scenarios set in Indian products (Swiggy, CRED, Zerodha, etc.).
3. After each answer, give: a score out of 5, what was good, what was missing, and a model answer in under 80 words.
4. After 6 questions, summarise my weak spots and suggest what to re-read.
```

### 2. Answer from memory
- Don’t look at the lesson while answering. The struggle to remember is what makes it stick.
- Type answers in full sentences, as you’d say them in an interview.

### 3. Do the checkpoint drill
On Friday, use this prompt for your Week 1 checkpoint:

```
I'm going to explain the four product risks out loud and type a transcript of it.
Then I'll explain why "build a chat feature" is a solution, not a problem.
Grade me like a hiring manager: clarity, accuracy, examples, structure, timing.
Point out anything vague. Then ask me one tricky follow-up question.
```

Record yourself on your phone first (2 minutes max), then type or paste what you said.

### 4. Save what you learn
- Copy the weak-spot summary into the notes section of the relevant lesson in PM Path.
- If a question stumped you, add it to your interview-prep page in Notion.

## Tips

- **Ask for scenarios**, not definitions: “Give me a situation and ask me which risk is highest.”
- **Ask it to argue back:** “Challenge my answer as a sceptical engineering manager.”
- **Vary the products** so you build range: fintech, quick commerce, B2B SaaS.
- **Don’t outsource the thinking.** Write your answer first, then ask for feedback. Reading AI answers without trying first teaches very little.
- **Check claims.** If it states a fact about a company (“CRED launched X in 2021”), treat it as unverified unless you can confirm it.
