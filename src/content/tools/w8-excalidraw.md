## What it is

**Excalidraw** (excalidraw.com) is the free hand-drawn-style whiteboard you used for your P3 architecture diagram. **Paper and a pen** work just as well. This week you’ll use either for **low-fidelity sketches**: the user flow and quick screen ideas, before opening Figma.

## Why PMs use it

Sketching is the fastest way to explore ideas and align with designers and engineers. Rough sketches invite feedback on structure (“what goes where?”) rather than colours. PMs who sketch communicate faster and waste less design time.

## Account setup (free plan)

- **Excalidraw:** open excalidraw.com; no account needed. Save your work as a `.excalidraw` file (Menu → Save to disk).
- **Paper:** A4 sheets folded into panels, a dark pen, and your phone camera.

## Walkthrough: flow and Crazy 8s for P1

### 1. Draw the user flow (15 minutes)
1. In Excalidraw, use **rounded rectangles** for start/end (double-click a rectangle → adjust roundness in the left panel), **rectangles** for screens, **diamonds** (press **D**) for decisions.
2. Start with the entry point(s) on the left, end states on the right.
3. Connect with **arrows** (A); label branches “yes/no” or “success/failure/pending”.
4. Add edge cases (empty, error, loading) as separate small boxes.
5. Save and export as PNG for your P1 case study.

### 2. Crazy 8s on paper (8 minutes)
1. Fold an A4 sheet in half three times to make 8 panels.
2. Set a timer for 8 minutes. Sketch one idea for your key screen in each panel: one minute each. Don’t polish; boxes and squiggles are fine.
3. Use different approaches: list vs. cards, one big number vs. breakdown, chat-style vs. form-style.
4. Photograph the sheet.

### 3. Pick and refine (15 minutes)
1. Circle the 2 most promising panels.
2. Redraw each as a phone-shaped frame in Excalidraw: draw a tall rounded rectangle (~360×720) and place boxes for header, content and the primary button.
3. Write **real content** (actual amounts, names, labels).
4. Add arrows between frames to show what each tap does.

### 4. Paper prototype test (optional, 15 minutes)
1. Print or draw each screen on a separate card.
2. Ask a friend to “use” it: they tap with a finger; you swap cards to show the next screen.
3. Note where they hesitate. This is a usability test at near-zero cost.

### 5. Share
1. Put the flow PNG and photos of sketches in your P1 Notion page under “Process”.
2. These “messy middle” artefacts show recruiters how you think.

## Tips

- **Speed over beauty.** If a sketch takes more than 2 minutes, it’s too detailed for this stage.
- **One idea per frame.**
- **Use a library**: Excalidraw has free community libraries (wireframe kits); click the library icon to add phone frames and UI shapes.
- **Annotate behaviour** with short notes and arrows.
- **Keep all versions**; they’re evidence of iteration for your case study.
